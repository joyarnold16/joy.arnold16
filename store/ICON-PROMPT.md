## If it comes back wrong, add these

Pick whichever matches the problem:

- too busy → `simpler, fewer elements, more empty space, poster-like`
- too soft / airbrushed → `flat vector illustration, hard edges, no airbrush`
- too pale → `deeper blacks, more saturated orange, stronger contrast`
- subject too big → `smaller subject, much more margin around it`
- too cartoonish → `serious industrial tone, not cute, not chibi`
- added words → `absolutely no text or lettering anywhere in the image`

---

## Checklist — reject anything that fails

1. **No text.** Any letters or numbers at all: reject. AI spells them wrong,
   and Play discourages text in icons anyway.
2. **Nothing touching the edges.** Android crops the icon to a circle. If the
   subject runs to the border, the crop eats it.
3. **Squint at it.** Shrink the image to the size of a thumbnail. If you
   cannot tell what it is, it fails — that is the size it actually appears at
   on a phone.
4. **One idea only.** A bomb, or a worker, or a blast. Not all three.
5. **Dark background.** It has to sit with the screenshots and the banner.
6. **Square**, and as large as the tool will give you — 1024x1024 or more.
