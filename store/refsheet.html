Blastgrid — art reference for an icon

Everything here is taken from the shipping build. Match these colours and this weight of shape.
THE GAME AS IT LOOKS
Raised tiles with a lit top-left edge and a dark
bottom-right edge, on a cool slate floor.
THE CHARGE & THE WORKER
Left: the charge, lit fuse. Right: the worker in
hi-vis and a yellow helmet. Blocky, few details.
PALETTE
Near-black and cool slate behind. One warm
light source. Saturated accents, never pastel.
SAFE ZONE
Android crops the icon to a circle or a squircle.
Everything that matters must sit inside the
dashed circle, with empty space to the edges.
