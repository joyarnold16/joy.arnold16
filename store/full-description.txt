Blast your way through a collapsing demolition site.

Drop a charge, get clear, and watch the blast tear a cross through the grid. Break the blocks, scrap the patrol drones, and find the lift before the clock runs out. Then do it again, one sector deeper.

THE SHOVE

Walk into a charge you have already placed and it slides. Shove one into a drone and the kill scores double. It is the shot nothing else in this genre gives you, and it turns a careful bomb maze game into something you can play aggressively.

WHAT YOU ARE UP AGAINST

• Patrol drones that hunt, drift and phase through walls
• Wooden pallets that catch fire and spread it
• Smoke that chokes you if you stay in it
• Concrete columns that come down if you hit them hard enough
• A clock, and a site that gets meaner every sector

EVERY RUN IS DIFFERENT

• Pick a perk between sectors — longer blasts, more charges, remote detonation, a reinforced vest, steel toes that launch a shoved charge
• Your furthest sector is kept, so a bad run never costs you everything
• A daily run on a grid that is the same for every player, with a day streak

BUILT TO BE PLAYED ANYWHERE

• Plays offline — the game needs no connection (ads and the optional revive do)
• One hand, four directions and a charge button
• No account, no sign-up
• Small download, runs on modest phones

Free to play, with an optional ad to get back on your feet after a bad run.

If you grew up on bomb maze arcade games — dropping bombs, blowing up blocks, cornering enemies in a grid — this is that, with a demolition crew, a patrol of drones, and a kick that actually pays.
