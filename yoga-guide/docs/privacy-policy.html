Privacy Policy for Yoga Guide

Last updated: 2026-08-10

Yoga Guide ("the app") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, and does not connect to the internet at all.

Health information. The app asks about pregnancy, blood pressure, injuries and similar conditions so it can remove unsafe poses from your sessions. That information is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is never transmitted, never seen by us, and cannot be, because the app has no network access.

Data collection

The app collects no personal data of any kind. It has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting, and no account or sign-in system. There is no server behind it.

Internet access

The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission and cannot make network requests. Every pose, illustration, session and audio cue ships inside the app itself. This is enforced by the Android permission system rather than by our policy, so nothing you enter can leave your phone even in principle.

Local storage

The app saves the following on your own device, using the device's standard on-device storage:

  • Your health profile: the conditions you selected and the health flags you ticked, plus your trimester if you indicated you are pregnant.
  • Your preferences: experience level, session length, voice, chime and vibration settings, and your reminder time.
  • Your practice history: the date, length and completion of each session, and any before/after symptom ratings you chose to record.

None of this leaves your device. It is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its storage, and you can erase all of it at any time from Settings → Erase all my data.

Permissions

  • VIBRATE — a short haptic cue at pose transitions and breath-phase changes. Grants no access to any personal data.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — used only for the one daily practice reminder, and only if you turn it on. The notification is generated on your device; nothing is fetched or sent.

The app requests no other permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or health platform data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date.

Not medical advice

Yoga Guide is educational. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting, particularly during pregnancy or when managing a diagnosed condition.
