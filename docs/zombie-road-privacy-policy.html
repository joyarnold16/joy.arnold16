Privacy Policy for Nanu Zombie Road

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Nanu Zombie Road ("the app") does not itself collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app shows optional rewarded ads (see "Advertising" below), which is the only reason it connects to the internet - the game itself, your progress, and your gameplay have no server, no account, and no analytics.

Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded video ads using Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): you can choose to watch an ad to revive after a run ends, to double the coins of a finished run, or for free coins on the home screen. Watching an ad is always your choice - declining it just leaves the game as it was. We don't send Google anything about how you play - no scores, no progress, nothing gameplay-related. Google's ad SDK operates under its own privacy policy and may itself collect device information and the device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads; see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information in ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Data collection

Nanu Zombie Road has no analytics, no crash reporting, no in-app purchases, and no account or sign-in system. It collects no personal data of its own; the only outside party involved is Google's ad SDK, described above.

Internet access

The app uses the Android INTERNET and network-state permissions solely so its ad SDK can request and display ads. No other part of the app makes network requests, and the game plays fully offline (ads are simply not offered then).

Local storage

The app saves your coins, upgrades, unlocked stages, best scores, and settings locally on your own device so your progress persists between sessions. This data never leaves your device, is never seen by us, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Other permissions

Other standard permissions the game engine requests (such as vibration and keeping the screen awake during play) are used only for gameplay on your device and do not give access to any personal data.

Children's privacy

Nanu Zombie Road is a zombie action game and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. It contains ads served through Google's Mobile Ads SDK, described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
