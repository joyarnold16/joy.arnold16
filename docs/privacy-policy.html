Privacy Policy for Nanu Blastgrid

Last updated: 2026-08-21

Nanu Blastgrid ("the app") does not itself collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app shows ads (see "Advertising" below), which is the only reason it connects to the internet at all - the game itself, your progress, and your gameplay have no server, no account, and no analytics.

Advertising

The app shows ads (an interstitial between runs, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch for an extra chance to continue a run) using Google's Mobile Ads SDK. We don't send Google anything about how you play - no scores, no progress, nothing gameplay-related. Google's ad SDK operates under its own privacy policy and may itself collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads; see Google's Privacy Policy for what that covers. Watching the rewarded ad is always optional - declining it just ends your run normally.

Data collection

Nanu Blastgrid has no analytics, no crash reporting, and no account or sign-in system. It collects no personal data of its own; the only outside party involved is Google's ad SDK, described above.

Internet access

The app requests the Android INTERNET permission solely so its ad SDK can request and display ads. No other part of the app makes network requests.

Local storage

The app saves your best score, sector reached, and similar progress locally on your own device (via the device's standard on-device storage, never transmitted anywhere) so your progress persists between sessions. This data never leaves your device, is never seen by us, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

The app requests only the VIBRATE permission, used solely for haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g. when an explosion goes off). This permission does not grant access to any personal data.

Children's privacy

Nanu Blastgrid is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. It contains ads served through Google's Mobile Ads SDK, described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
